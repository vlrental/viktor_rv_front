# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Customers in British Columbia who want to rent an RV, plus rental owners and VL Rental administrators who manage bookings.

## Product Purpose

Help customers discover rentals, create an account with Google or email/password, book securely, and return to their booking history. Give administrators role-protected access to booking operations.

## Brand Personality

Local, dependable, outdoorsy. The experience should feel personal and trustworthy rather than corporate or marketplace-like.

## Anti-references

Do not copy Drako World's visual identity, generic SaaS login screens, glassmorphism, or dense enterprise dashboards. Drako is an architecture reference only.

## Design Principles

- Keep the rental journey primary; authentication supports booking rather than interrupting browsing.
- Show clear ownership, dates, prices, and booking state.
- Preserve the established VL Rental forest, gold, photography, and typography system.
- Make account and error states understandable without technical language.
- Use the same sign-in surface for customers and administrators; permissions come from server roles.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA contrast, keyboard access, visible focus, reduced-motion support, responsive layouts, and explicit form labels and errors.
